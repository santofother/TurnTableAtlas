# S31 · Corivessa and Corivessa Port on the Known World

Status: DONE

- **Miniatures on the map.** Corivessa (×12, 1.1 units across) and Corivessa Port (×10, 0.93 units) now show their own `city.glb` on the Known World. `Core/TownSite.cs` and `Game/Town.cs` lay every vertex onto the relief and keep its height above the model's ground. No hole is cut in the relief.
- **Blending (reworked after review, like Karn Ashur's basin):** `TownSite.Rim` defines a rounded, noise-ragged rim. Along it the model's hills (above its own plain) die out, trees and fields thin out in groves, and `town_ground.gdshader` hands over to the map's colour and light. Inside the rim the town's ground is graded towards the map's palette, 30% in the walls and 95% in the countryside. The model's seabed sinks under the map's water.
- **Water:** `town_water.gdshader` draws the port's harbour in the map's own sea colour, sampled at its mouth, and dithers it out along the rim. The Thennara runs through Corivessa. Elsewhere the map's own coast shows.
- **Surroundings:** the S30 farmland is no longer flat pale cards. `marches_fields.gdshader` shades each plot from the map's colour under it, with crop strips and holdings and ragged, dithered edges. The plots follow the relief. Farmsteads, tents and the marches road were cut to the towns' scale (the road was ~800 m wide). Roads and farms keep out of each town's rim.
- Opt-in: `world_map.miniature: {exaggeration}` on a plain city (both configs and packages; `docs/city_schema.md`). `--towns=off` hides the miniatures.
- **Minor pin.** Corivessa Port's settlement type is `city` in `known_world.json`, so `PinTiers` treats it like Flamemouth. It is hidden from the overview and appears once about one realm fills the view. Capitals, the temple-city and metropolises stay major.
- **Arrow keys pan** the orbit target like a right-drag (Shift = faster), on the map and in cities. They are skipped while a slider or list has focus. Hints and the README list them.
- New views: **Views → Corivessa** and **Corivessa Port** (`CAM_Town_<id>`).

**Check:** `tools/run_tests.sh` is green: 52 xUnit tests (TownSite ×5, a new pin-tier case), Python and seven self-test runs. The self-test now checks both miniatures, the minor port pin and an arrow-key pan. Stills: `S31_corivessa_port_map.jpg`, `S31_corivessa_map.jpg`. Also try `$GODOT --path atlas -- --view=CAM_Town_corivessa_port`.
