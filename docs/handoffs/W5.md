**Approach:** an Atlas-side hero patch. The blend's heights are 3.2 km/px, so the valley would only have been ~6 px
there. `KnownWorld.blend` is unchanged, and Blender renders of it still show the old cone. Open question, default
taken: the blend's site is kept, and the basin is centred on `settlement:karn_ashur`.

**Landed**
- `Core/KarnAshurSite.cs` (pure layout) and `Core/ReliefSampler.cs` (the relief as the GPU draws it). The patch is a
  basin in the old peak. Floor at 2,900 m, a lake ring round an island, cliffs that rise to ~3,100 m in the south and
  peaks of ~4,750 m with snow in the north, six waterfalls, and a gorge to the south. The great peak is a spur on the
  west wall, with the Temple of the First Flame at 3,700 m (the lore's height) and an 8-leg switchback road up to it.
  The city is a ~6x miniature: 44 wall towers, 4 gates with banners, bridges, a 5-tier ziggurat and two smaller ones,
  ~4,200 houses, ~6,400 trees, 3 aqueducts on arches fed by the falls, and a field patchwork with canals and farmsteads.
- `Game/KarnAshur.cs` and the shaders `karn_ashur_{ground,water,fall,flame,stone}`. Windows light up at night, the
  fires always burn, and torches line the road. The first build takes ~0.5 s and is cached between visits.
- `relief.gdshader` has a `hole` uniform that sinks the relief vertices under the patch; the patch meets the ones on
  the edge exactly. `WorldMap.cs` replaces the map collision under the patch, and `KwPoint` uses the patch's heights.
  The settlement glow fades out close up. New preset: Views → "Karn Ashur" (`CAM_KarnAshur`). New options:
  `--orbit=x,y,z,yaw,pitch,dist` (a fixed camera) and `--karn-ashur=off` (the relief alone, for before shots).

**Check:** `tools/run_tests.sh` is green: 6 new xUnit tests (seam, rim, pass, road, placement, layout) and a self-test
line. `W5_before_after.jpg` shows before | after (top: mid-distance; bottom: the Karn Ashur view). `W5_karn_ashur.jpg`
