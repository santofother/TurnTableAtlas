Calderra's 73 placed boats have recurring voyages in Atlas and in new Blender
cinematic runs. Sixteen pier and quay boats wait 45–80 seconds between trips.
The generated city manifest supplies the same paths, speeds and phases to both
apps. The Atlas Ships layer controls the placed boats and lane traffic.

Twelve miniature ships sail five routes on the Known World. The routes connect
Calderra, Salmara, Dovehall, Ossmere port and Corivessa port. The two port labels
are the existing lore placeholders. Routes in shipping_routes.json use Known
World blend XY units and are checked against the baked relief.

The Atlas world map uses the Known World relief. The painted Geography map is
retained as an authoring reference for legacy city pin coordinates.
