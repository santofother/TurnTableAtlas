**Landed** (blend edited by `tools/kw_edits/w2_terrain.py`, then rebaked into `atlas/assets/map/known_world/`)
- **Oravian Expanse:** ridged hills and low mountains (polygon between Astera, Madravella and Calloway). They rise
  northwards into the Veryan Range, capped softly at ~1,500 m (default answer to the open question). River valleys
  (major and minor), lakes and towns are masked out, so the rivers, lakes and cities stay on the ground. The Expanse
  stops west of `HERO_GreatGate`.
- **Dovaran Crown** (land = kingdom id 7): the islands are lifted into a rugged 300–900 m highland, recut with
  136 fjord arms (long, narrow, steep-walled, 150–400 m deep, some branching) that stop short of each island's core
  and of each other, plus ~300 skerries. Dovehall is untouched: no lift within ~0.6 u and no fjord within ~1.1 u, on
  both the base terrain and the `hero_Dovehall_z.exr` patch.
- **Also edited** (the coast needs them): `albedo_land.png` and `feature_masks.png` alpha (the land masks), cleared
  in the fjords; `water_data.png` fjord pixels set to channel water (no shallows or reef). Every draped mesh (terrain,
  TLODs, hero patch, roads, villages, rivers, lakes, cities, tree canvas) shifts by the same height change.
- **Backups:** `KnownWorld.2026-11-23.bak.blend` and `textures/pre_w2/` (originals of the five edited images).
  The script re-reads those originals and each mesh's `w2_z0` attribute, so re-running it gives the same result.

**Check:** `docs/handoffs/W2_before_after.png` (top: CAM_07 before/after; bottom: top-down baked Dovaran Crown

**Left / notes**
- Heights are 3.2 km/px, so the fjords are 2–3 px wide in the Atlas. The hero patch carries them finer in Blender.
- Gotcha: the terrain objects show scale z = 15 but `matrix_world` is identity. Mesh z = metres / 16093.44 locally.
- W3: the dark hero rectangle at Dovehall is unchanged. If W3 edits `water_data.png`, don't re-run W2 afterwards
  (it restores the `pre_w2` copy first).
- Lift tuned to read at map scale. Dovehall's own island stays lower and greener than the others.
