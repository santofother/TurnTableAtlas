card: W4 (Salmara–Calderra shared border; every other realm-pair gap closed)
status: DONE
date: 2026-11-23

**Where borders come from:** the blend's `T_Kingdoms` (`textures/kingdoms_id.png`, 3425x1785, Non-Color). R = realm id
(0 unclaimed, 1 Calderra, 2 Astera, 3 Salmara, ...), G = distance to the nearest border (x16 per px, capped at 255),
which `M_Terrain`'s `S4_*` nodes turn into the border line and tinted band. The bake draws them into `albedo.png`. The
provisional geojson it was rasterised from isn't anywhere on disk, so the texture is the source.
`FOMSMap/political-overlay` (the painted map) builds regions from shared border lines and never had the gap.

**Cause:** the valley of `RIVER_river_border` ("Border river", tributaries W1/W2 and an upland lake outlet) was left
unclaimed: a 10–20 px corridor from the coast to the Veryan foothills, with branches up the tributaries.

**Landed:** `tools/kw_edits/w4_borders.py` first fills that corridor (2,304 px). Each pixel goes to the nearest realm it can
reach without crossing the rasterised river, then the river pixels go to the nearer bank. The river is the line
(default answer): Salmara holds the west bank (+1,365 px), Calderra the east bank (+618), and Astera the east bank of
the upland outlet (+321, opposite Calderra's northern lobe). G is recomputed only where the edit moves it. Rebaked
`atlas/assets/map/known_world/`. Backups: `KnownWorld.2026-11-23-pre_w4.bak.blend`, `textures/pre_w4/` (the script
always starts from there, so re-running gives the same result). `-- --dry-run` and `-- --preview <prefix>`
are available.

**Check:** `docs/handoffs/W4_before_after.png` (baked albedo, before | after). `tools/run_tests.sh` is green.
`$GODOT --path atlas -- --map --map-mode=known_world`

**Other realm pairs (closed too, on request):** the script then finds every other unclaimed strip between two realms
(land a 20 px closing of the claimed area would fill, 50 px or more, touching two or more realms) and gives each
pixel to the nearest neighbour (4-connected flood). Pixels on the 3425x1785 id map:
- Eastern Marches / Varduun: 1,373 px (EM +1,197, Varduun +176; a bay, mostly Eastern Marches now)
- Kervara / Valdora: 752 px (Kervara +521, Valdora +231)
- Dovaran Crown / Kervara: 520 px (+254 / +266, on the strait coast)
- Astera / Madravella: 241 px (+125 / +116)
- Coastal Sultanate / High Dunes: 172 px (+79 / +93)
- Astera / Calderra: 98 px (+38 / +60, the foothill notch north of the river)
After the run: 0 strips between realms left. Before | after crops: `docs/handoffs/W4_other_gaps.png`
(left column top to bottom: the first three; right column: the last three).
The unclaimed land north of the realms (Veryan/Orthean foothills) stays unclaimed on purpose, as in the political
overlay. So does a small unclaimed knob on Salmara's coast that touches only Salmara.
