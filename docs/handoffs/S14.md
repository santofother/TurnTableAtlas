The Known World has permanent fog west of Vesperra's marked line and along
southern Quadria. Separate fronts roll through the Vaelwood and Thrasia on
75- and 90-second cycles, lingering longer in foggy weather.

Fifteen new water-checked routes cover the River Flame delta and six northern
Quadrian ports, Caelora and Quadria to Hespera and Serotina, arrivals under
Vesperra's southern cape, and eastern arrivals at Corivessa and Ossmere ports.
The delta berth is just offshore north of Haven, which sits inland.

The 54 ships use a smaller trireme miniature with a square sail, three banks
of oars, a swept stern and a bronze bow. At full-map zoom the hulls are about
one-third their former length, with a minimum size for close inspection.

with --view=CAM_10_WholeWorld and watch the north and east for a minute.
