# S30 · Valdora, Corivessa Port and the Great Gate

Status: DONE

- The Known World view **Valdora · western marches** follows the route from the Calderran frontier to the port, inland to the capital and a short distance east into Ventara. The road, camps and fields follow the relief mesh, with marsh pools concentrated in the lower ground.
- The port has 917 houses and 18 waterfront buildings, six piers, a rocky headland, reeds and a tent camp at the west road. Bright waterfront lanterns and merchant traffic give the quay activity after dusk.
- The inland capital has 1,143 houses, a hilltop palace, a toll office, a walled market and the Great Gate across the Thennara. The river and the Canyon Road pass under the masonry arch.
- Both configs build through `tools/build_city.sh`. Use `corivessa_port` or `corivessa_great_gate` as the argument. Each package uses the existing Known World settlement and the corresponding painted-map dot or star.
- To visit, start the Atlas and choose **Views → Valdora · western marches**. Click a city pin. Both city panels have named close-ups. The regional camera ID is `CAM_ValdoranMarches`.
- Checked terrain, routes and placement in Blender anchor maps and Atlas stills. The manifests cover harbour arrivals, festival processions, the border track and the road east. See the [three scene renders](../../blender/renders/valdora/).
- `tools/run_tests.sh` passes Python and C# tests, the Atlas build and seven headless city/map round trips.
