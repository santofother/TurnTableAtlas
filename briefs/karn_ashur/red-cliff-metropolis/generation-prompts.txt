01-caravan-approach

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a standalone wide panoramic photorealistic environment concept painting for a preindustrial fantasy atlas. The image references show the earlier red-cliff Karn Ashur design; use them for red layered cliff geology, adobe architecture, sunlight and desert/snow contrast. The new plan supersedes their modest town size. Karn Ashur is a very large inland sacred caravan metropolis of roughly 500,000 permanent residents, built up in dense connected neighborhoods over a footprint about 6 km across, with about 300,000 more people dispersed over the vast High Dunes region. Scale roof and figure sizes realistically. Warm ochre mudbrick, red sandstone retaining walls, compact two- to four-story courtyard houses, dusty road, oasis greenery with dry land alongside, huge striated red cliffs, two enormous rocky mountains with snow-covered tops and sand drift aprons at their bases. The higher western peak carries a tiny distant flame sanctuary. At the foot of that peak is Al-Mirah, a modest spring shrine and a few palms, with a separate long arduous pilgrimage climb. Two peak tributaries descend into the basin, meet below the urban core and exit south/southeast. Thin human-made irrigation channels take water sideways to field strips on the lowest ground. Neighborhoods on elevated benches and shelves, grain warehouses and several walled caravan courtyards by the freight road, no giant palace, no city-scale circular enclosure, no sea at Karn Ashur, no modern objects, no text, no frame. Favor clear afternoon daylight with enough even exposure to read the terrain. Preserve the visual scale of an ocean of roofs and city infrastructure, with unbuilt channels and floodplains discernible.
View: View from the northwestern desert caravan road on a low sand-covered ridge, 24 mm landscape. Both peaks appear behind the city, their skyline readable despite perspective. Active foreground caravan freight yards with numerous animals, unloading porters, storehouses and cloth awnings naturally arranged at the dry outskirts. Much larger layered metropolis spreads well beyond the nearest market across the midground into the distance to both sides, dense near-continuous roofs. There is no giant wall across the view. The traveler sees the whole urban extent, not a small market standing in for the city. Clearly show rock and sand at the foot of the snow mountains.
